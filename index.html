Vigor is your health. Eat to restore it. Warmth drains at night, in the cold and in the Gloam. Stand by a Hearth or Lantern, or eat Emberberries, to warm up. If warmth runs out, you start losing vigor.

Earn embers from almost everything you do. Each level lets you pick a perk. Take on daily bounties, raid ruins for blueprints and loot, farm, and brace for Umbral Nights every fourth night.

Gloomlings come out in darkness and won't step into bright light, so light up your home. A Hearth also becomes your respawn point. Follow the goal in the top left.
